About the data & method

Facts and relations are separate layers: corpus.json (facts), relations.json (inference). Build documents: corpus_report.md (phase 1 — extraction & merges), adjustments.md (phase 2 — vocabulary reconciliation), MODELS.md (phase 3 — the relational models), ARCHITECTURE.md (phase 4). Nothing here was authored by hand at the presentation layer; all views are generated from the data modules.

Keyboard: 1–5 switch views · / search · v cycle verification filter · Esc close detail.

Fact layer from the six SWE reports only — no invented titles, authors, years or identifiers. Edges are report-stated, derived (quoted basis), or marked EDITORIAL. Citation rule throughout: title · author/body · year of last publication.
